About Us

Improving the Pickleball Playing Experience

Business Background

PickleLevel is a sports technology startup focused on improving the pickleball playing experience through a mobile application. It was developed to address the difficulty of matching players with different skill levels. The app allows players to assess their abilities through a short questionnaire, categorize their skill levels, and connect with players who have similar abilities.

Mission

To provide pickleball players with an easy and reliable way to identify their skill levels and connect with players of similar abilities, promoting fair, balanced, and enjoyable games.

Vision

To become a trusted player-matching platform in the pickleball community, making the sport more accessible, organized, and enjoyable for players of all skill levels.

Main Features

Core Application Features

Player Registration

Quick onboarding profile creation allowing players to set up their sports bio, preferred play times, and local home courts.

Skill Assessment Questionnaire

A quick, accurate self-evaluation questionnaire diagnosing player shot control, dinking, serving consistency, and match experience.

4-Tier Skill Categorization

Automatic categorization of players into Beginner, Novice, Intermediate, or Advanced skill brackets.

Skill-Based Player Matching

Intelligent player connection algorithms ensuring you are matched with players of similar abilities for balanced, competitive games.

Open-Play Session Information

Real-time information on active open-play sessions, local court availability, schedules, and active player counts in your area.

Pro & Clubs

Premium Application Features

Skill Progress Tracking

Track your athletic improvement over time as your questionnaire scores and win ratios advance.

Matching History

Access detailed records of past matches, partners played with, scores, and match balance ratings.

Advanced Preferences

Customize match filters including strict skill radius, singles/doubles preference, and indoor court options.

Session Management

Tools for players and sports organizations to schedule, organize, and manage balanced club open-plays and sessions.
